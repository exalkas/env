## 📚 **Carbon Markets 101 - Learning Path Structure**

### **Module 1: Introduction to Carbon Markets**

**Objective**: Understand what carbon markets are and why they exist

**Lessons:**

1. **What are Carbon Markets?** (Text + Video)

   - Definition and purpose
   - Environmental context and climate change
   - Types of carbon markets (compliance vs voluntary)

2. **History of Carbon Trading** (Text)

   - Kyoto Protocol origins
   - Evolution to Paris Agreement
   - Key milestones and developments

3. **Market Participants** (Interactive)

   - Governments and regulators
   - Companies and compliance entities
   - Traders and intermediaries
   - Verification bodies

### **Module 2: European Union Allowances (EUA) Fundamentals**

**Objective**: Deep dive into EUAs as the primary carbon instrument

**Lessons:**

1. **What are EUAs?** (Text + Video)

   - Definition and legal framework
   - One EUA = One tonne CO2 equivalent
   - Allocation vs auctioning

2. **EU ETS System Overview** (Interactive)

   - Covered sectors and installations
   - Cap-and-trade mechanism
   - Banking and borrowing rules

3. **EUA Lifecycle** (Text)

   - Issuance and allocation
   - Trading and transfer
   - Surrender and cancellation

### **Module 3: Market Mechanics and Trading**

**Objective**: Understand how carbon trading actually works

**Lessons:**

1. **Trading Venues and Platforms** (Text)

   - Exchanges (ICE, EEX)
   - OTC markets
   - Brokers and market makers

2. **Price Discovery and Factors** (Video + Interactive)

   - Supply and demand fundamentals
   - Weather and energy prices
   - Policy announcements impact
   - Economic indicators

3. **Basic Trading Strategies** (Text)

   - Hedging for compliance
   - Speculation and arbitrage
   - Risk management basics

### **Module 4: Regulatory Framework**

**Objective**: Understand the rules and compliance requirements

**Lessons:**

1. **EU ETS Phases and Evolution** (Text)

   - Phase 1 (2005-2007): Pilot phase
   - Phase 2 (2008-2012): Kyoto period
   - Phase 3 (2013-2020): Centralization
   - Phase 4 (2021-2030): Market Stability Reserve

2. **Compliance and Monitoring** (Interactive)

   - Monitoring, Reporting, Verification (MRV)
   - Registry systems
   - Penalties for non-compliance

3. **Recent Reforms and MSR** (Video)

   - Market Stability Reserve mechanism
   - Free allocation rules
   - CBAM introduction

### **Module 5: Market Analysis and Data**

**Objective**: Learn to analyze and interpret carbon market information

**Lessons:**

1. **Key Market Indicators** (Interactive)

   - Price charts and trends
   - Volume and open interest
   - Volatility measures

2. **Fundamental Analysis** (Text + Video)

   - Supply-demand balance
   - Verified emissions data
   - Auction results interpretation

3. **Information Sources** (Text)

   - Official EU sources
   - Market data providers
   - News and analysis platforms

### **Module 6: Practical Applications**

**Objective**: Apply knowledge to real-world scenarios

**Lessons:**

1. **Case Study: Power Sector** (Interactive)

   - How utilities use carbon markets
   - Fuel switching decisions
   - Pass-through to electricity prices

2. **Case Study: Industrial Compliance** (Text)

   - Manufacturing company compliance strategy
   - Cost management approaches
   - Investment decisions

3. **Getting Started in Carbon Trading** (Video)

   - Career paths and opportunities
   - Required skills and knowledge
   - Next steps for learning

- **Estimated Duration**: 8-10 hours
- **Prerequisites**: None
- **Target Audience**: New traders, compliance officers, sustainability professionals
- **Assessment**: Quiz after each module + final comprehensive test
